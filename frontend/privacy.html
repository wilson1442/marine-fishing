Introduction

Angler Suite ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Angler Suite marine fishing intelligence platform. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website and use our services. Please read this policy carefully. By accessing or using our platform, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

Personal Information You Provide

  • Name, email address, and region when you submit our access request form
  • Account credentials (name, email, password) when you create an account
  • Messages or comments you submit through our contact form
  • Catch reports and fishing data you voluntarily submit

Information Collected Automatically

  • Browser type, operating system, and device information
  • IP address and approximate geographic location
  • Pages viewed, features used, and time spent on the platform
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies for session management

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To provide, operate, and maintain our platform and services
  • To process your access requests and manage your account
  • To display catch data, weather information, and vessel tracking on our maps
  • To respond to your inquiries and provide customer support
  • To send you updates, newsletters, or promotional materials (with your consent)
  • To improve our platform, features, and user experience
  • To detect and prevent fraud, abuse, or security incidents
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms

Data Sources

Our platform aggregates publicly available data from the following sources:

  • NOAA Fisheries (catch and species data)
  • NOAA NDBC (weather buoy observations)
  • NOAA CO-OPS (tide predictions and water levels)
  • NOAA MRIP (marine recreational information program)
  • AIS vessel tracking data (publicly broadcast ship positions)
  • ERDDAP satellite data (sea surface temperature, chlorophyll-a)

This publicly sourced data does not contain personally identifiable information.

Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies to manage user sessions and authentication. These are essential cookies required for the platform to function. We do not use third-party advertising or analytics cookies. You can configure your browser to refuse cookies, but some features of the platform may not function properly without them.
